Avijit Chowdhury

I am a Postdoctoral Fellow at the Indian Institute of Astrophysics (IIA), Bengaluru. My research investigates how dark matter, accretion flows, plasma, and other astrophysical environments modify black hole dynamics and observable signatures. I develop analytical and computational models of perturbations, accretion, tidal response, and ringdown, aiming to connect strong-field gravity theory with multi-messenger observations.

Research Interests

Black Hole Perturbations & Spectroscopy

Developing analytical and computational studies of quasinormal modes, ringdown, stability, echoes, and perturbations of black holes and compact objects to test gravity in the strong-field regime.

Environmental Effects

Investigating dark-matter halos, accretion flows, plasma effects, and their influence on black-hole dynamics, transonic accretion, and how these environments imprint on gravitational-wave observables.

Modified Gravity & Compact Objects

Studying quasinormal modes, causality, stability, massive modes, and exotic compact objects in modified-gravity theories beyond General Relativity, including Einstein-Gauss-Bonnet and quadratic gravity.

Tidal Response & Multimessenger Gravity

Studying tidal deformation and Love numbers of accreting compact objects and connecting strong-field calculations with joint electromagnetic and gravitational-wave signatures.
